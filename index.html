I'm the Site Administrator at a 560-unit luxury residential tower in Toronto, working under a professional property management firm. My days move between resident communications, financial administration, compliance enforcement, vendor coordination, and board reporting — the quiet infrastructure that keeps a large building running smoothly.

Before this role, I worked as a Head Office Administrator across a portfolio of a dozen or more buildings, which gave me a wide view of how different properties are run before I moved into this site-specific position.

I'm currently completing my Condominium Manager designation through Humber and CMRAO coursework, working toward becoming a fully licensed manager at a luxury building.
